How the score shuffle works +

Each team has two players and nine holes. On each hole, the higher of the two player scores is the team score; those nine scores make the team total. A steal exchanges two individual scores — it never copies or deletes one. Swaps happen in log order, so a score can move more than once. Lower totals rank first; tied totals share a rank.

Player 1 is the first player listed for that team in the scorecards CSV; Player 2 is the second. Use the timeline to return to any point in the round.
